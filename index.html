Jetson AGX Xavier — Module Anatomy

An interactive breakdown of the Jetson AGX Xavier system-on-module: explore the SoC, power delivery, memory subsystem, and interconnect the way you'd walk a new hire through a real board.

About this Training

This module walks through the physical anatomy of the Jetson AGX Xavier compute module — the same board you'd inspect during bring-up, thermal validation, or field debugging. Rather than reading a datasheet cold, you can rotate the actual module, click into any populated zone, and read the engineering context behind it: why it's shaped the way it is, what fails first, and how it connects to everything else on the board.

  • Identify the Xavier SoC package and its six integrated compute engines (CPU, GPU, NVDLA, PVA, and more).
  • Recognize the VRM bank's multi-phase power delivery stage and why phase count matters under load.
  • Locate the LPDDR4x memory and PMIC cluster and understand its role in shared CPU/GPU memory access.
  • Understand the high-density edge connector as the module's single mechanical and electrical link to a carrier board.
  • Recognize mounting-standoff torque as a root cause of uneven thermal throttling in the field.
